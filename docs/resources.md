# De Anza resources

Verified facts the Resources assistant may use. If something is not here, say so and point the student to the official page. Do not invent hours, rooms, or emails.

## Free food

Roary's Market (the campus food pantry, formerly called the Food Pantry) is open to current De Anza students. You need a De Anza student ID (physical or digital) and to be registered for the current term. You can visit once per week. Bring a bag.

It is inside the Hub in the Baldwin Winery building. The entrance is on the north side, facing the A Quad and Stevens Creek Boulevard. Financial Aid is on the other side of the building.

Fall quarter hours from the official page:

- Monday: 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
- Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday: 10:30 a.m. to noon and 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.
- Friday: closed

Questions: [dafoodpantry@deanza.edu](mailto:dafoodpantry@deanza.edu) or [Roary's Market](https://www.deanza.edu/resources/hub/roarys-market/).

Other food on campus, from the same page:

- Village Centers have snacks, soup, oatmeal, coffee, and tea for registered students in need during fall, winter, and spring quarters, Monday–Thursday 10:30 a.m.–3 p.m. See [food sites around campus](https://www.deanza.edu/resources/hub/roarys-market/locations.html).
- West Valley Community Services Park-It Market: Parking Lot A, second and fourth Tuesday, 1:45–3 p.m. Bring student ID and a bag.
- Loaves & Fishes: Parking Lot A, Thursdays 2–3 p.m. Bring a bag.
- CalFresh: monthly grocery help on an EBT card. The Hub can help you apply. Second Harvest: 1-800-984-3663, weekdays 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

## Bikes

DASG Bike Program: qualified students can borrow a bike or e-bike (plus helmet, lock, and corral key) for the quarter at no cost.

Requirements listed on the official page: 18 or older, current DASG card, enrolled in at least 6 units, and all fees paid or on a Cashier's Office installment plan.

Request form: [DASG Bike Program](https://www.deanza.edu/collegelife/dasgbikeprogram.html).

## Computers

From [Need a Computer?](https://www.deanza.edu/students/computers):

- The Library checks out laptops, Chromebooks, iPads, calculators, and Wi-Fi hotspots. Loan length depends on the device.
- Desktop computers are in the Library reference area and the Library Computer Lab (LC 113), including Adobe Creative Cloud and Microsoft 365. Printing is available in the Computer Lab.
- CompTechS (Occupational Training Institute) may give eligible students a free refurbished computer.
