## Free food

Source: [Roary's Market](https://www.deanza.edu/resources/hub/roarys-market/) · Last checked: Oct 2026

Roary's Market (the campus food pantry, formerly called the Food Pantry) is open to current De Anza students. You need a De Anza student ID (physical or digital) and to be registered for the current term. You can visit once per week. Bring a bag.

It is inside the Hub in the Baldwin Winery building. The entrance is on the north side, facing the A Quad and Stevens Creek Boulevard. Financial Aid is on the other side of the building.

Fall 2026 hours from the Roary's Market page:

- Monday: 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
- Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday: 10:30 a.m. to noon and 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.
- Friday: closed

The older Hub page lists different Monday hours. If a student asks about Monday, suggest they confirm on the Roary's Market page.

Questions: [dafoodpantry@deanza.edu](mailto:dafoodpantry@deanza.edu) or [Roary's Market](https://www.deanza.edu/resources/hub/roarys-market/).

### Other food on campus

Source: [Food sites around campus](https://www.deanza.edu/resources/hub/roarys-market/locations.html) · Last checked: Oct 2026

- Village Centers have snacks, soup, oatmeal, coffee, and tea for registered students in need during fall, winter, and spring quarters, Monday–Thursday 10:30 a.m.–3 p.m.
- West Valley Community Services Park-It Market: Parking Lot A, second and fourth Tuesday, 1:45–3 p.m. Bring student ID and a bag.
- Loaves & Fishes: Parking Lot A, Thursdays 2–3 p.m. Bring a bag.
- CalFresh: monthly grocery help on an EBT card. The Hub can help you apply. Second Harvest: 1-800-984-3663, weekdays 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

## Bikes

Source: [DASG Bike Program](https://www.deanza.edu/collegelife/dasgbikeprogram.html) · Last checked: Oct 2026

DASG Bike Program: qualified students can borrow a bike (plus helmet, lock, and corral key) for the quarter at no cost. **E-bikes are currently unavailable.** The page says they are expected back by Winter 2027.

Requirements listed on the official page: 18 or older, current DASG card, enrolled in at least 6 units, and all fees paid or on a Cashier's Office installment plan. The last day to enroll in an installment plan is the second Friday of the quarter.

To request a bike, fill out the form on the DASG Bike Program page for the current quarter. You log in with your CWID and MyPortal password. You are responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged equipment, and there is a $10 late fee for each business day an item is overdue, up to the replacement cost.

## Computers

Sources: [Need a Computer?](https://www.deanza.edu/students/computers) and [Library Technology Lending](https://libguides.deanza.edu/DATech) · Last checked: Oct 2026

- The Library checks out laptops, Chromebooks, iPads, calculators, and Wi-Fi hotspots. Loan length depends on the device. Some laptops are day-use only and must be returned the same day.
- To borrow, you need a valid student ID and must be enrolled with fees paid. Laptops, tablets, and hotspots require signing a Technology Borrower Agreement.
- Wi-Fi hotspots are free for students enrolled in the current term and are loaned for the term.
- Laptops and tablets have waitlists. Day-use items do not.
- Desktop computers are in the Library reference area and the Library Computer Lab (LC 113), including Adobe Creative Cloud and Microsoft 365. Printing is available in the Computer Lab.
- CompTechS (Occupational Training Institute) may give eligible students a free refurbished computer.
